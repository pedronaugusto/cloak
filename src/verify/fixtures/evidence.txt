Scope and interfaces
Strict borrowed certificate parsing: certificate.parse(DER, Limits) ParseError!Certificate;
certificate.parsePublicKey(SPKI) supplies validated RSA/PSS, P-256/P-384 and Ed25519 metadata.
Adjacent namespace entries are src/certificate.zig, src/wire.zig and src/verify.zig.
types.Verification directly owns receipt vocabulary and storage; Verification.zig is an alias.
verify.verify(allocator, Request, anchors) and verify.indexed(allocator, Request, *const Issuers)
return owned request-bound receipts. Issuers owns its sorted entries but borrows anchors and DER;
the snapshot owner retains those through index deinit. Original anchor-policy indices survive
sorting. Unusable unrelated anchors are omitted; all retained alternate issuers remain candidates.
verify.nativePath(allocator, Request, OS_selected_chain) checks exactly the supplied chain.
The native driver must establish OS policy success with retrieval disabled first. This function
neither rebuilds anchor trust nor overrides OS distrust. Leaf serial, issuer metadata, validity,
SAN/purpose/pins, constraints, policy and supplied offline evidence receive the same portable floors.
Portable path capacity follows available peer candidates plus one anchor; native capacity follows
selected-chain length, including OS intermediates absent from Request.chain.
